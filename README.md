### Solutions Architect · Senior Software Engineer

.NET & Distributed Systems · Baku, Azerbaijan · Open to new opportunities

## About

I am a Solutions Architect with 9+ years of .NET / C# engineering, including 5+ years designing and delivering distributed, scalable, mission-critical enterprise systems in the financial and insurance sector.

My work focuses on:

- **Solution & enterprise integration architecture:** target architectures, integration strategy, and shared platforms
- **Microservices & event-driven systems:** asynchronous messaging, REST / gRPC APIs, real-time communication
- **Legacy modernization:** migrating monolithic and SOA systems to distributed, microservices-based landscapes
- **Identity & security:** SSO, authorization servers, Keycloak
- **Engineering excellence:** OOAD, TDD, code base standards, mentoring, and technical interviewing

I am a Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT) and hold MCSD, MCSA, and MCP certifications. I also write about .NET and distributed systems as an author at C# Corner.
